1. What is Artificial Intelligence?

Artificial Intelligence (AI) is a technology that enables computers and machines to perform tasks that normally require human intelligence.

AI can learn, solve problems, recognize images and understand language.

2. History of AI

  • 1950: Alan Turing introduced the idea of machine intelligence.
  • 1956: The term Artificial Intelligence was introduced at the Dartmouth workshop.
  • 1997: IBM Deep Blue defeated chess champion Garry Kasparov.
  • 2010s: Deep Learning and intelligent assistants became popular.
  • 2020s: Generative AI and ChatGPT transformed technology.

3. Types of AI

Narrow AI

AI designed to perform specific tasks.

Example: Siri, Google Maps

General AI

AI that could perform different intellectual tasks like a human.

Not achieved yet.

Super AI

A hypothetical AI that would exceed human intelligence.

Does not currently exist.

6. Benefits and Limitations

Benefits

  • Saves time
  • Reduces repetitive work
  • Improves productivity
  • Helps in decision-making

Limitations

  • Can produce incorrect information
  • Privacy concerns
  • May contain bias
  • Requires data and computing resources
