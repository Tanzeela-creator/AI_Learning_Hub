Generative AI

Artificial intelligence that can write, draw, compose and code. Learn how it works, where it is used and what to watch out for.

🤖 What is Generative AI?

Generative AI is a type of artificial intelligence that creates new content instead of only analysing existing data. It can write text, draw images, compose music, produce video and generate computer code. Tools such as ChatGPT, Claude and Midjourney are well-known examples.

⚙️ How Does It Work?

A generative model is trained on a very large amount of data, such as books, websites, images or code. During training it learns patterns, for example which words usually follow each other or what shapes make up a face. When a user types a prompt, the model uses those patterns to produce new content that fits the request.

  1. Training: the model studies huge amounts of data.
  2. Learning patterns: it finds links between words, shapes and sounds.
  3. Prompt: the user asks for something.
  4. Generation: the model creates new content that matches the prompt.
💬

Large Language Models

Work with text and code. They predict the next word again and again to build sentences.

🎨

Diffusion Models

Work with images. They start from random noise and slowly shape it into a picture that matches the prompt.

⚖️ Benefits and Risks

✅ Benefits

  • Saves time on repetitive work.
  • Helps people come up with ideas and overcome a blank page.
  • Makes learning and creative tools available to more people.

⚠️ Risks

  • Hallucinations: confident but wrong answers, so facts must be checked.
  • Bias: models can repeat unfair patterns from their training data.
  • Copyright: open questions about who owns AI-made content.
  • Deepfakes: fake images, voices and videos can mislead people.
  • Over-dependence: relying on AI too much can weaken your own thinking.

🔮 The Future

Generative AI is improving quickly and is becoming part of everyday tools such as search engines, office software and phones. In the future it may act as a personal assistant for studying, working and creating. At the same time, rules, honest use and fact-checking will become more important so that people can enjoy the benefits while avoiding the harm.
